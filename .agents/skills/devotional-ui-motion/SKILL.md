# Devotional UI & Motion Design Skill

This skill governs the aesthetic and interactive feel of the Bible application:

## Design Principles
1. **Calm Typography**: High legibility Tamil (`Noto Sans Tamil`) and English (`Lora`) serif fonts with comfortable line height (1.6 - 2.1).
2. **Subtle Motion**: Fade & slide chapter transitions (`fadeInSlide`), warm verse highlight pulse when bookmarked.
3. **Accessibility**: ARIA labels, semantic landmark elements, keyboard shortcuts (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`, `Ctrl+K`, `Esc`).
